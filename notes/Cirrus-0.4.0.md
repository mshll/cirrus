
Math in your notes, a new icon, and a panel that gets out of the way.

- End a line with `=` to see its result, in the accent colour. Name a value with `name = 12 * 3` and use it below, or add up the lines above with `total =`, `sum =`, `average =`, or `count =`. Units, dates, time zones, and currencies at daily exchange rates all work. Click a result to copy it, or turn math results off in Editor settings.
- A new icon, with proper dark and tinted versions.
- The panel now hides when you click away. Turn on Keep Open, formerly Keep on Top, to keep it up. If you turned the old setting off, it stays off.
- Turn iCloud sync off for this Mac in Settings. Changes made meanwhile sync once you turn it back on.
- Capture Clipboard saves in the background and confirms with a toast, without pulling you out of the app you are in.
- Typing into a peek from the edge pill goes into the note once the pointer is over it.
- Clicking the edge pill hides the panel in one click, and brings it over from another display or Space.
- Empty lines between blocks stay put when you reopen a note.
- Trash retention and the new-note template sync between your devices.
- Note titles drop Markdown syntax and skip image lines.
- New installs sort notes by last edited.
- Acknowledgements for the open source Cirrus builds on, in About.
