
Math in your notes, and a new icon.

### New

- End a line with `=` to see its result. Variables, totals, units, dates, time zones, and currencies all work. Click a result to copy it.
- A new app icon, with dark and tinted versions.
- Turn iCloud sync off on a single Mac.

### Improved

- The panel hides when you click away, unless Keep Open is on.
- Capture Clipboard saves in the background.
