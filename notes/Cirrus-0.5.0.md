
A panel in your colours, and one click from the menu bar.

### New

- Tint the panel in Settings › General: blue, green, purple, or rose, each in a dark and a light shade that follow your appearance, or any colour you pick. Text switches between light and dark to stay readable on it.
- Set the panel's opacity from glass to solid, so text stays legible over bright windows. Increase Contrast makes it solid.

### Improved

- Clicking the menu bar icon shows or hides the panel. Right-click for the menu, or swap the two clicks in Settings › General.
- The menu bar menu is down to what you reach for: New Note, Capture Clipboard, Paste Mode, Settings, Check for Updates, and Quit.
- The menu bar icon matches the app icon.
- Math results update faster as you type in long notes.

### Fixed

- Math that reads the clock, like `time in portland =`, updates every minute instead of keeping the time it was typed.
