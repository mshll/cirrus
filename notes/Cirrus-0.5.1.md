
Cirrus opens again on macOS 26, and now runs on macOS 14 and 15 too.

### New

- Cirrus runs on macOS 14 Sonoma and macOS 15 Sequoia, from the download and the Mac App Store.

### Improved

- Rest the pointer on the pill or notch and the peek takes the keyboard, so typing goes into your note instead of the app underneath.

### Fixed

- Cirrus 0.4.0 to 0.5.0 could quit at launch on macOS 26. If yours won't open, download it again from trycirrus.app.
- Browse opens on the note you're in, scrolled into view, without jumping.
- The Edge Pill preview in Settings draws the pill and notch the way they look on screen.
