
Crash reports, on your terms.

### New

- If Cirrus quits unexpectedly, it offers to email the crash report. Nothing is sent unless you send it.
- Copy Diagnostics in Settings › About, for bug reports.
- Open the command palette from any app with a shortcut you set in Settings › Shortcuts.

### Improved

- The edge pill and notch are on for new installs.
- The welcome note introduces math, links, and tags.
- The command palette can hide and quit Cirrus, open pinned notes, and show, hide, or move the edge pill.
