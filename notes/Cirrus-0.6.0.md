
Your notes, your look, on the display you want.

### New

- Settings › Panel › Open on picks the display the panel opens on: the last one, the pointer's, the active window's, or a specific one.
- Move Panel › Next Display, in the command palette and as a global shortcut.
- Choose the text and code fonts.
- Choose the edge pill's position, color, and display.
- An Appearance pane in Settings gathers every visual setting.

### Improved

- The edge pill rests as a thin line and grows into a tab on hover. Increase Contrast makes it thicker.
- The hover tab stays open while its note is docked.
- Code block tools, the language picker, and the link list match the native look.

### Fixed

- Hiding the menu bar icon no longer crashes Cirrus.
- The panel keeps its height and its spot on each display when you move it or rearrange your displays.
- A code block's first line no longer sits under its tools.
- The onboarding pill preview follows the app's appearance.
