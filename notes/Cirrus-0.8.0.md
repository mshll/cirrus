
Native bars and menus, and a lighter panel.

### New

- On the Mac the formatting bar, link bar, and `[[` note list are native Liquid Glass, and never hide under the panel's header or footer.
- A right-click menu built around what you clicked: link, image, code block, table, and task actions, then spelling, Look Up, Translate, and Paste as Plain Text.
- Paste as plain text with ⇧⌘V.
- Settings › Appearance › Text weight. Picking a face in the font panel sets it too.
- The formatting bar's tooltips show each button's shortcut.

### Improved

- Cirrus does less work while you type, switch notes, and sync, and long notes and code blocks stay responsive.
- Floating bars and menus spring open from the text they sit on.
- Launch no longer slows down as crash reports from other apps pile up.

### Fixed

- The panel no longer moves on every launch.
- A panel you dragged away from its pill stays put when the Dock resizes, a display wakes, or the resolution changes.
- A docked panel fits a shorter display, and the height refits after you move the panel to another display or turn the edge pill off.
- Reset Panel Size and Position turns dynamic height back on.
- The caret no longer goes under the footer's blur when you type at the bottom of a note.
- An unfinished frontmatter edit is no longer lost when you switch notes.
- The panel's header and footer hide when the pointer leaves, even through a card over its edge.
- A device with sync paused or signed out no longer empties trash that another device restored.
- A rare crash in the command palette.
