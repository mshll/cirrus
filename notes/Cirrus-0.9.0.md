
Your shortcuts, and a fresh note when you want one.

### New

- Change any shortcut in the panel in Settings › Shortcuts › In the Panel, or clear it.
- Settings › Panel › Open a new note: never, after 5 minutes away, or every time the panel shows.
- Turn Keep Open on and off from the panel's header.

### Improved

- Swiping with two fingers follows the same history as ⌘[ and ⌘], and right after launch both go back through the notes you opened last time.

### Fixed

- ⌘[ and ⌘] go through notes in the order you visited them, without repeats or skips.
- Return at the end of a folded heading adds a heading below the section and keeps it folded.
