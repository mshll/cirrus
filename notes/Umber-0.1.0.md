
Introducing Umber: notes that float above everything on your Mac. Press ⌥⌘N in any app, write it down, and press Escape to get back to work.

### New

- Sync between your Macs through your own iCloud, with no account.
- Peek at notes from a pill on the screen edge or the notch.
- Markdown with checklists, highlights, images, code, and tables.
- Link notes with `[[Note Title]]`.
- ⌘K runs any action, and ⌘P finds any note.
- Clipboard capture, Paste Mode, and Services.
- Shortcuts, Siri, URL scheme, and Spotlight.
- Markdown export and daily backups.
- Free for three notes. Umber Pro unlocks unlimited notes with a one-time purchase.
