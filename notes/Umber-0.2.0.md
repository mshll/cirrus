
A first launch above the clouds, a faster palette, and a panel that moves like part of your Mac.

- The first launch rolls clouds over your screen, sets up your shortcut, iCloud, login, and the edge pill, then parts the clouds onto your notes and shows you how to hide and bring them back. Replay it from General settings.
- New installs show and hide notes with ⌃⌘N. If you already use Umber, your shortcut stays as it is.
- The panel opens on a spring, drops out of the notch, grows out of the edge pill, and casts a proper shadow.
- Settings are rebuilt in the style of System Settings, with a choice of accent colours.
- ⌘P turns what you typed into a new note with Return, renames and pins with ⇧⌘R and ⇧⌘P, and opens on the note you had before. ⌘K matches group names and keywords too.
- Recently Deleted shows when each note goes for good, and a new action deletes the checked tasks in a note.
- ⌘G and ⇧⌘G find the next and previous match.
- Headings 4 to 6 with ⌘4 to ⌘6. ⌃⌘↑ and ⌃⌘↓ move any block. Tab indents code, lists start from `+` and `1)`, and Backspace at the start of a heading or quote unwraps it.
- Typing `[[` closes the link for you and offers to create a note that does not exist yet. ⌘L inserts a link when nothing is selected, and typing a Markdown character around a selection applies it.
- Copy or save an attached image from its toolbar.
- The menu bar shows when Paste Mode is on, and adds Capture Clipboard and Browse.
- VoiceOver reads the palette, the footer, and the edge pill.
- Many fixes for sync conflicts, keyboard layouts, display changes, and editing edge cases.
